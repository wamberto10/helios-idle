# HELIOS IDLE — Forja e Reator

Modo: image_gen embutido, uma chamada por asset, fundo transparente solicitado.
As artes locais extrator.png e refinaria.png foram inspecionadas para orientar a descrição visual.
Saídas: PNG RGBA de 1254 × 1254 pixels. Transparência real conferida por leitura do canal alpha (mínimo 0, máximo 255).
Os prompts abaixo são transcrições exatas enviadas à ferramenta.

## Forja — assets/buildings/forja.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one isolated transparent PNG building sprite for HELIOS IDLE, a polished mobile science-fiction construction game.
Style/medium: clean hand-painted 2D game illustration with crisp dark contours, readable beveled metallic volumes, controlled glossy edge highlights and restrained texture. Match a coherent family of machines made from warm ivory armor plates, deep navy machinery, brushed steel and bronze/gold joints, with small luminous cyan accents. Slightly elevated three-quarter isometric view, showing the front, right side and top, lit from upper left. Chunky clear silhouette readable at small game scale.
Composition: square canvas, exactly one complete machine centered, every part fully visible, approximately 75% canvas occupancy with generous truly transparent margins on all four sides. The object sits on its own integrated metal feet or machine chassis; no separate ground tile. Keep all shadows and luminous effects inside the machine silhouette.
Constraints: real transparent alpha background, not a colored or checkerboard backdrop. No scenery, horizon, terrain, platform tile, external cast shadow, people, text, numbers, labels, UI, logo, border or watermark.
Primary request: a compact futuristic forging module, visually distinct from a refinery. A stout industrial press/hammer is visibly aligned over a small heavy anvil, integrated into a compact ivory-and-navy machine frame with bronze clamps and hydraulic joints. A short recessed channel carries glowing orange molten metal toward the anvil. Orange heat is visible only inside the channel and working chamber; small cyan windows and indicators complement it. Prioritize a readable press-plus-anvil silhouette and sturdy compact construction, with a few pipes and mechanical details rather than a dense factory. One coherent machine, not multiple separate assets.
```
